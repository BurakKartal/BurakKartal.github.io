Block Bloom – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Block Bloom (“the app”, “we”) is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information is processed when you play.

Information stored on your device

Your scores, settings, game progress and purchase status are stored only on your device. We do not run servers and we do not collect this information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt,
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location,
  • device information (model, OS version, language),
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks),
  • diagnostic data.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Choices inside the app. You can also turn off tracking in iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple’s leaderboards under your Game Center profile. This is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

A support email address will be published here soon.
